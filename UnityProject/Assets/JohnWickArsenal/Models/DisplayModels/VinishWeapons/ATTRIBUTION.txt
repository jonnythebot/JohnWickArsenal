## Important Notes
1. These are **generic models**, not exact replicas of AR-15, AWP, MP5, or Remington 870
2. They are visually similar and suitable for cosmetic display purposes
3. For exact real-world models, user must provide Sketchfab URLs
4. Textures are embedded in GLB files (no separate texture files needed)
5. Models use +Y up, front faces +Z, origin on ground

## Quest Optimization
- All models under 15K triangles (well under Quest limits)
- GLB format with embedded PBR textures
- Ready for glTFast import in Unity
