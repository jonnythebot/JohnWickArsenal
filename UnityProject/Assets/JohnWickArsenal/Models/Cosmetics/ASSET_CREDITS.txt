John Wick Arsenal cosmetic source models

ClassicBlackSuit.obj adapts Margaret Toigo's Suit with Tie and Jacket mesh.
DoubleBreastedSuit.obj and DinnerJacket.obj adapt her male double-breasted
suit and suit with dinner jacket meshes. The original garments and textures
are licensed under CC0 1.0. This project converts their units to meters,
sets the garment origins, and supplies 1K textures and Unity OBJ materials.
Source: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits01.html
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Briefcase.fbx adapts the BriefCase model and textures from Cases by
loafbrr_1. This project re-exports the model with relative 1K albedo and
normal texture paths. The original assets are licensed under CC0 1.0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/cases
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The garments are static meshes for preview and wardrobe authoring. Applying
them to characters requires skin weights, fit checks, and wardrobe entries for
the intended character rig. The source OBJ and MTL placeholders are preserved
under Backups/ProceduralModels/Suits.

Asset import inventory

ClassicBlackSuit: replaces the procedural ClassicBlackSuit OBJ/MTL in
Models/Suits. The original object name and ClassicBlackSuit_Wool slot
are retained. The original source files are under Backups/ProceduralModels/Suits.
The replacement has 19,202 triangles and 1K albedo and height textures.

DoubleBreastedSuit: new static wardrobe-authoring source, 10,062 triangles,
1K albedo and tangent-space normal maps.
DinnerJacket: new static wardrobe-authoring source, 15,300 triangles,
1K albedo and tangent-space normal maps.
Briefcase: new static cosmetic prop, 712 triangles, 1K albedo and normal maps.

The OBJ materials reference albedo. Normal and height maps accompany
the OBJ files as authoring inputs. The briefcase FBX references both maps.
Unity's Android importer uses ASTC 6x6 and a 1024-pixel ceiling for these
textures. Verify texture connections and performance in Unity and on Quest.
